      📚 Health Tips

      🤱 Breastfeeding

      Feed on demand, maintain hydration, and ensure a good latch.

      🧼 Cord Care

      Keep dry, avoid covering with diapers, and consult if signs of infection appear.

      🍽️ Nutrition

      Start solids at 6 months, offer a variety of foods, and avoid added sugars.

      🚑 First Aid Tips

      Monitor fever, provide sponge baths, and seek help if temperature exceeds 38.5°C.

      🏠 Home Care Tips

      Encourage rest, keep child hydrated, and monitor symptoms regularly.
